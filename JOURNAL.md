# EVA: Security Framework

## What is it?

Im Building the Hardware which would Connect to the Security Framework and show the stats/alerts/ directly onto the screen without looking at a terminal. the Design is inspired by the MAGI system of Evangelion. It would show on the OLED Screen about the Ads the Firewall blocked and the trackers destroyed. when someone is attacking a network whether by a DDOS attack or anything else. it would show a RED ALERT on the Screen. there would be a Button on the device which would remove the access to internet from the computer.

Today I worked on the Schematic a little bit, I looked up the Components I would need to build this and everything else. I picked RP2040 because its very powerful and really great microcontroller, I also added the mandatory Flash and LDO regulator. a bunch of resistors and capacitors and USB C.
